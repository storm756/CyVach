### Labelling protocol (exact instruction used for every batch)

> You are labelling SMS messages for the CyVach taxonomy. Read `docs/label_guidelines.md` (v1, 2026-10-17) and apply its decision procedure in order: spam (fraud, credential or money theft, malware, OTP-sharing requests, OTP-style texts from personal numbers) > caution (legitimate security-sensitive: OTP or verification code, login/new-device alert, password/PIN change, unusual-activity alert) > promo (non-deceptive commercial marketing) > ham (personal chat and non-sensitive notifications). Use only the sender and text shown; ignore the placeholders <NAME>, <PHONE>, <URL> and so on. For each id, output one line: `id<TAB>label<TAB>confidence(1-3)<TAB>borderline(0/1)<TAB>reason (≤12 words)`. Every id exactly once.

The test-set decision (owner, 2026-10-18): the owner declined fully blind manual labelling. Claude's blind proposals are the test labels, and a human check of a random subset by the owner is pending the owner's choice (default: about 200 items, accept/override, override rate reported). Limitation to state in the paper: the test labels are AI-proposed, with only partial human verification.

Stress sets (2026-10-18): base templates and Hinglish messages written by Claude Opus 5.5; generator `src/make_stress.py`; evaluation only.
